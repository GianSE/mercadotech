Formal employment in Information Technology (IT) in Brazil has grown rapidly over the past two decades, yet the available analyses of the sector rely on closed aggregates or short time windows, and cannot be reproduced. Following CRISP-DM, this work built an analytical platform over the microdata of the Annual Social Information Report (RAIS) and the General Register of Employed and Unemployed Workers (CAGED), from 2007 to 2026. Its Medallion architecture (Bronze, Silver and Gold) runs entirely in the cloud at no cost: the data lake is a set of public Hugging Face Hub repositories, processing is done by GitHub Actions workflows that reprocess only the years with new data, and the web dashboard queries the Apache Parquet files with DuckDB in the visitor's browser. A total of 661~GB of text microdata was processed and stored as 56.7~GB of Parquet; the technology scope, defined as the union of economic activity (CNAE) and occupation (CBO), comprises 23.8 million RAIS employment records and 12.7 million CAGED movements. Recounting the scope from the raw data stored in the Bronze layer ended with zero difference in every year, and the totals fell within about 1\% of those in a public panel published by the Ministry. The audit found defects in the source itself, such as the civil service of the state of Rondônia declared as systems programmers in eight years, whose correction reversed the trend of the gender gap. Applied to the IT labor market, the platform shows that the stock of jobs grew 162\% from 2007 to 2025, in waves; that 58\% of IT professionals work outside IT companies, although growth was concentrated in them; that median pay in minimum wages remained stable; and that the racial wage gap narrowed while the gender gap emerged after 2015. For forecasting, a nowcasting estimator of the employment stock was off by 1.1\% on average, against 1.6\% for the direct accounting identity and 5.1\% for the naive estimator, and SARIMA outperformed the seasonal naive baseline for the monthly balance. The processed layers are published as open data.\\

\textbf{Keywords}: IT labor market; data engineering; RAIS; CAGED; open data.
